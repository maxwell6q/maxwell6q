### 🔭 Current Work
Writing my Master's Thesis on *Developing Methods for Energy Efficient Gaits in Quadruped Robots* in collaboration with the German Aerospace Center (DLR).
In this work I am employing advanced Optimization and Reinforcement Learning techniques to minimize the Cost of Transport of the DLR's quadruped robot Ebert. My work focusses on:
* **Optimization:** Trajectory optimization of a parametrized Central Pattern Generator (CPG) using Genetic Algorithms.
* **Reinforcement Learning:** Training robust control policies in a nature inspired approach of modulating the CPG parameters directly instead of joint angles.
* **System Dynamics:** Investigating the exploitation of non-linear modes for passive energy efficiency.
